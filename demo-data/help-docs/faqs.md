# DemoKart: Frequently Asked Questions

DemoKart is an online shop in India selling electronics, home and kitchen items, clothing and footwear.

## Delivery

### How long does delivery take?

Metro cities: 2 to 4 business days. All other areas: 4 to 7 business days. You see the expected date at checkout.

### Is delivery free?

Delivery is free on orders of Rs. 499 or more. Orders below Rs. 499 have a delivery fee of Rs. 49.

### Do you deliver outside India?

No. DemoKart delivers only to addresses in India.

### How do I track my order?

Go to My Orders and tap Track. Once your order ships, we also send a tracking link by SMS and email.

### Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?

Yes, but only before the order ships. Go to My Orders, open the order and tap Change address.

## Payments

### Which payment methods do you accept?

UPI, credit and debit cards, net banking, DemoKart Wallet and Cash on Delivery.

### Do you offer Cash on Delivery?

Yes, on orders up to Rs. 10,000. Cash on Delivery has a fee of Rs. 30.

### How do I apply a coupon code?

At checkout, tap Apply coupon and enter the code. Only one coupon can be used per order.

### Is my payment information safe?

Yes. Payments go through a certified payment gateway, and DemoKart never stores your full card number.

### What is DemoKart Wallet?

DemoKart Wallet is your store balance. Refunds can go to it instantly, and you can use it to pay for any order.

## Orders

### How do I cancel an order?

Before it ships: go to My Orders and tap Cancel. After it ships you cannot cancel, but you can refuse the delivery or return the item.

### Can I order without creating an account?

Yes, you can check out as a guest. To track a guest order, use your order ID and phone number on the Track Order page.

### Do you offer gift wrapping?

Yes. Gift wrapping costs Rs. 39 per item. Choose it at checkout.

## Returns

### What is your return policy?

Most items can be returned within 10 days of delivery if they are unused and in their original packaging. Some items cannot be returned; see the Returns and Refunds Policy.

### How do I return an item?

Go to My Orders, open the order and tap Return. A courier picks the item up from your address within 2 to 3 business days.

### When will I get my refund?

After the returned item passes our quality check: UPI and DemoKart Wallet in 1 to 2 business days, cards and net banking in 5 to 7 business days.

### Can I exchange an item for a different size or colour?

Yes, for clothing and footwear, within 10 days of delivery. Each order can be exchanged once.

## Problems

### My item arrived damaged. What should I do?

Report it within 48 hours of delivery: go to My Orders, tap Report a problem and add photos. We will send a replacement or give a full refund.

### I received the wrong item. What should I do?

Report it within 48 hours of delivery: go to My Orders, tap Report a problem and add a photo of what you received. We will collect it and send the right item.

## Support

### How do I contact customer support?

Chat with us in the app or on the website, 9 am to 9 pm every day. You can also email support@demokart.example.
