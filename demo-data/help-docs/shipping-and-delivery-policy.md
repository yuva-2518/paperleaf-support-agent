# DemoKart Shipping and Delivery Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

## Where we deliver

DemoKart delivers to addresses in India only. We do not ship to other countries.

## Delivery times

| Area | Delivery time |
|---|---|
| Metro cities (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Pune, Ahmedabad) | 2 to 4 business days |
| Other cities and towns | 4 to 7 business days |
| Remote areas (Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Lakshadweep, Ladakh, and parts of the North-East) | 7 to 12 business days |

Business days are Monday to Saturday, not counting public holidays.

## Same-day delivery in Goa

Orders placed before 12 noon with a delivery address in Panaji, Margao, Mapusa or Vasco da Gama can arrive the same day. Same-day delivery costs Rs. 99 and is shown at checkout when it is available. Large appliances are not available for same-day delivery.

## Delivery fees

- Orders of Rs. 499 or more: free delivery.
- Orders below Rs. 499: Rs. 49.
- Same-day delivery in Goa: Rs. 99 extra, on any order value.

## Large appliances

Large appliances are delivered by our own team, who also set up the item where needed. We call you the day before to fix a time slot.

## Missed deliveries

If nobody is available, the courier tries twice more on the next business days. After three missed attempts, the order is returned to us and cancelled, and you get a full refund.

## Delays

If your order has not arrived 3 days after the expected date, contact support. We find out what happened and, if the parcel is lost, send a replacement or give a full refund.
