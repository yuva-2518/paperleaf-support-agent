# DemoKart Warranty and Repairs Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

## Brand warranty

Electronics and appliances sold on DemoKart come with the brand's own warranty. The length is shown on each product page (usually 1 year). Keep your DemoKart invoice: the brand's service centre asks for it. You can download the invoice from My Orders.

## DemoKart Assured: extra 6 months

Items with the **DemoKart Assured** badge get an extra **6 months** of warranty from DemoKart, starting when the brand warranty ends. You do not need to register for it.

## What the warranty does not cover

- Physical damage, such as cracks or dents
- Water or liquid damage
- Damage from power surges or the wrong voltage
- Repairs done by anyone other than the brand or DemoKart
- Normal wear, such as scratches or a weaker battery over time

## How to make a warranty claim

- **Within the brand warranty:** contact the brand's service centre. The address and phone number are on the warranty card in the box and on the product page.
- **Within the DemoKart Assured extra 6 months:** go to My Orders, open the order and tap Warranty claim. A technician contacts you within 2 business days. We repair the item, or replace it if it cannot be repaired.

## Items that are faulty on arrival

If an item does not work when it arrives, report it within 48 hours of delivery through My Orders, Report a problem. This is handled as a return, not a warranty claim, and you get a replacement or a full refund.
