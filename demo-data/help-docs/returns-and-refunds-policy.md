# DemoKart Returns and Refunds Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

## Return window

Most items can be returned within **10 days of delivery**. The item must be unused, with all tags, and in its original packaging, with everything that came in the box.

Large appliances (washing machines, refrigerators, air conditioners, televisions over 43 inches) have a **7-day** return window and are returned only if they are faulty or damaged on arrival. A technician visits first to check the item.

## Items that cannot be returned

- Innerwear, swimwear and socks
- Earphones, earbuds and headphones once the seal is opened (for hygiene reasons)
- Beauty and personal care products once opened
- Food, groceries and other perishable items
- Gift cards and DemoKart Wallet top-ups
- Items marked "Not returnable" on the product page

Damaged, faulty or wrong items can always be reported within 48 hours of delivery, even if they are on this list.

## How a return works

1. Go to My Orders, open the order and tap Return. Choose a reason.
2. A courier picks the item up from your address within 2 to 3 business days. Keep the item packed and ready.
3. Our team checks the item within 2 business days of pickup (the quality check).
4. If the item passes the check, the refund starts straight away.

If the item fails the quality check (for example, it is used or parts are missing), we send it back to you and no refund is made. We tell you why by email.

## Refund times

Refunds start after the item passes the quality check.

| How you paid | Where the refund goes | Time |
|---|---|---|
| UPI | Your UPI account | 1 to 2 business days |
| DemoKart Wallet | DemoKart Wallet | 1 to 2 business days |
| Credit or debit card | Your card | 5 to 7 business days |
| Net banking | Your bank account | 5 to 7 business days |
| Cash on Delivery | DemoKart Wallet, or your bank account if you add it | 1 to 2 business days (Wallet), 5 to 7 business days (bank) |

Delivery fees and the Cash on Delivery fee are refunded only if the item was damaged, faulty or wrong.

## Exchanges

Clothing and footwear can be exchanged for a different size or colour within 10 days of delivery, once per order, if the new size or colour is in stock. If it is out of stock, we refund you instead.
